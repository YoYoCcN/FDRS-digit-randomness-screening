# Methodology note

FDRS evaluates whether decimal digit patterns in raw numerical data show non-random structure.

## Core modules

1. Decimal digit extraction
2. Single-decimal-digit distribution testing
3. Joint decimal-digit combination testing
4. Entropy and KL divergence
5. Digit-preference indices
6. Progressive subsampling stability analysis
7. Dataset-level feature engineering
8. Semi-supervised machine-learning risk scoring

## Risk interpretation

FDRS risk grades reflect digit-pattern irregularity:

- Grade 0: no apparent digit-pattern irregularity
- Grade 1: mild irregularity
- Grade 2: moderate irregularity
- Grade 3: high irregularity
- Grade 4: very high irregularity

These grades should not be interpreted as direct evidence of fabrication or misconduct.
